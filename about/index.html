Since 1998 in Kotkapura

The Structural Backbone For India's Toughest Sectors.

Founded in 1998, Narindra Techno Tools Corporation began with a clear mandate: provide tradesmen, workshops, infrastructure engineers, and heavy fabricators with uncompromising machinery that never fails under load.

Operating from our physical trade counter near the new bus stand (opposite Chopra Petrol Pump in Kotkapura, Punjab), we have served thousands of industrial enterprises, construction firms, and precision machining workshops across Punjab, Haryana, Delhi NCR, and nationwide.

We maintain direct, authorized relationships with the world's most prestigious toolmakers — Bosch, Makita, DeWalt, Milwaukee, Hilti, ESAB, Mitutoyo, Stanley, and Taparia. Every unit dispatched from our depot is verified for authentic factory tolerances.
